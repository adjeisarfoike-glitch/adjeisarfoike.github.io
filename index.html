Strategy on one side, a drawing hand on the other

I work across strategic communication, design, and digital media writing. My background is in publishing studies; my graduate work is in digital media communication at Texas State University, where I handle web content and communications for the School of Journalism and Mass Communication.

That combination is the useful part. I can build the campaign argument and then draw it, which means the message and the image are not negotiated across two desks. Most of what is on this site started as a communication problem before it became a poster.

Recent work runs from CSR communication research to the Save Asaase Yaa series on illegal mining in Ghana. I am open to collaboration on campaign work, brand identity, and editorial projects.
